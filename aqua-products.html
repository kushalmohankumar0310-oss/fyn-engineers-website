How It Works

Integrated Air Source Heat Pump

High-efficiency thermal transfer technology extracting heat energy from surroundings

FYN Aqua's Air Source Heat Pump Water Heating System utilizes proven high-performance, energy-efficient technology to deliver reliable hot water while significantly reducing operating costs.

The system is unique because approximately 75% of the heat energy is extracted from the surrounding air, while only 25% is supplied by electricity, making it an environmentally friendly and highly cost-effective water heating solution.

Coefficient of Performance (COP): Deliver a COP of 3.5 to 4.0, producing 3.5 to 4 units of heat energy while consuming only 1 unit of electrical energy!

Working Process & Vapor Cycle

The system operates on the vapor compression refrigeration cycle, using a refrigerant to absorb heat from the surrounding air and transfer it to water efficiently:

  • Evaporator: Absorbs ambient air heat through refrigerant-filled coils.
  • Compressor: Compresses refrigerant vapor to raise pressure and temperature.
  • Condenser: Heat exchanger transferring thermal energy directly to the water.
  • Expansion Valve: Reduces pressure/temperature before returning to start the cycle.
