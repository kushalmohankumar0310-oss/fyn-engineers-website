Solar Rooftop Systems

Our Rooftop Solar Options

On-Grid, Off-Grid, and Hybrid power system installations

On-Grid Solar Rooftop System

Solar panels generate electricity during the day to power building loads. Excess generated energy is exported back to the utility grid through net metering.

  • Maximum electricity bill savings
  • No battery bank maintenance required
  • Lower installation cost compared to battery systems
  • Ideal for areas with reliable grid power
  • Fastest return on investment

Off-Grid Solar Rooftop System

Solar panels charge a high-durability battery bank. The stored energy provides power during the night or during grid outages.

  • Complete electricity independence
  • Reliable standby power backup
  • Perfect for remote locations without grid connectivity
  • Ideal for rural homes, farmhouses, and remote offices

Hybrid Solar Rooftop System

Combining the best of On-Grid and Off-Grid. Integrates solar panels, battery banks, and the utility grid for optimal savings and continuous power.

  • Reduces energy bills while securing power backup
  • Provides automatic backup during grid failure
  • Intelligent power routing and energy management
  • Recommended for hospitals, hotels, and offices
