Our Capabilities

End-to-End Engineering Services

We follow a strict 5-stage engineering lifecycle to deliver sustainable infrastructure for our clients.

01

Engineering Design

We analyze geographical factors, roof tilt, shadowing, water TDS parameters, and electrical load curves to design blueprint drawings.

02

Procurement

We source certified parts from trusted OEMs and brand them under strict compliance and warranty frameworks.

03

Project Execution

Our in-house engineers manage construction, wiring, and integration on site, minimizing structural disruption.

04

Commissioning

Rigorous stress-testing of grids, filters, and backup transfer switches under maximum load configuration before handoff.

05

Maintenance & AMC

Dedicated tracking of warranty. Free services while active, followed by nominal annual service agreements for ultimate peace of mind.

Join Our Engineering Team

We are always looking for passionate engineers, designers, and maintenance experts who believe in site-level precision. At FYN, you'll be solving real utility problems on the ground.

Solar PV System Designer

Design Dept | Full-time | Bangalore HQ

Role Overview:

Responsible for analyzing site survey data (shading, angles, loading) and generating CAD blueprints for custom roof-mounted and ground-mounted solar grid installations.

Requirements:

B.E./B.Tech in Electrical/Energy Engineering, proficiency in AutoCAD/PVSyst, and 2+ years of utility-scale solar layout design experience.

On-Site Operations Lead

Field Operations | Full-time | Bangalore & Travel

Role Overview:

Lead the physical construction, wiring, structural integration, and OEM checklist compliance during commissioning of utility systems on location.

Requirements:

Diploma/Degree in Mechanical/Civil Engineering, 3+ years of project installation site management, and willingness to travel for client site integration.

Water Treatment Engineer

Water Solutions Dept | Full-time | Bangalore HQ

Role Overview:

Analyze site raw water diagnostic chemical data, configure filtration system stages (UF, RO, UV, Softeners), and monitor compliance indicators.

Requirements:

M.Sc/B.Tech in Chemical/Environmental Engineering, 2+ years of designing industrial-scale reverse osmosis or membrane filtration plants.

Graduate Engineering Trainee - Power Backup Systems

Power Dept | Internship / Trainee | Bangalore HQ

Role Overview:

Assist senior engineers in calculating critical client appliance loads, designing custom double-conversion UPS backups, and verifying storage banks.

Requirements:

Recent graduate in Electrical Engineering, strong theoretical knowledge of power electronics, inverter design, and lead-acid/lithium cell metrics.

Service & Support Technician

Customer Care | Full-time | Bangalore (Field)

Role Overview:

Conduct warranty-compliant regular servicing, troubleshoot circuit and filtration flow issues on site, and execute nominal post-warranty AMC repairs.

Requirements:

ITI/Diploma in Electrical/Instrumentation, 2+ years of field troubleshooting, and excellent client communication skills.
